# Mirro — Approved Brand Guide

## Source of truth
The visual reference is:
`reference/approved-brand-board.png`

Do not redesign or reinterpret the logo.

## Mark
The approved mark is a mirrored M made from two facing panels:
- left: deep primary blue
- right: light blue
- strong white negative-space M in the center
- rounded outer corners
- angled upper shoulders
- no glow, no extra outline, no decorative effects

The production raster assets were extracted directly from the approved board so the Android app matches the approved concept.

## Palette
- Primary Blue — #2563EB
- Light Blue — #60A5FA
- Charcoal — #0F172A
- Light Gray — #E2E8F0
- Background — #F8FAFC
- Surface — #FFFFFF

## Typography
- Primary: Inter
- Android alternative: Roboto
No font files are bundled in this handoff.

## Tagline
Same apps. More possibilities.

## Rules
Do not:
- introduce purple/cyan AI gradients
- change the M geometry
- add glassmorphism
- add glow/neon
- rotate the mark
- replace the approved icon with a generic duplicate-app icon
